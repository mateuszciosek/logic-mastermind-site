Binairo · one puzzle a day

A daily logic puzzle with hints that teach the technique, not just the answer. Every hint names the deduction it used and shows where it applies, so each puzzle leaves you a little better at the next one.

The first puzzle type is Binairo (also known as Takuzu or Binary Sudoku): fill the grid with rings and dots so that no three of a kind touch in a line, every row and column has an equal count, and no two rows or columns are identical.
