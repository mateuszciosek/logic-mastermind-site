Logic Mastermind

Eight papers · three puzzles a day · a twist at the weekend

A daily logic puzzle with hints that teach the technique, not just the answer. Every hint names the deduction it used and shows where it applies, so each puzzle leaves you a little better at the next one.

The paper prints three puzzles a day from eight: Binairo (Takuzu), Futoshiki, Sudoku, Star Battle, Tents, Nonogram, Skyscrapers and Battleships. Each has a two-minute lesson, a daily edition numbered from 1 August 2026, and hints written in its own words. On Saturdays and Sundays a fourth page, the twist, prints one paper under one extra rule.
