Logic Mastermind

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 September 2026

Logic Mastermind is an Android puzzle app published by Mateusz Ciosek ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to have it deleted. It is written to be read, not skimmed: the whole thing takes about three minutes.

The short version

  • The app has no account, no sign-in and no way to enter personal information. Your puzzle progress, streak and settings are stored only on your device.
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase so we can see which puzzles and hints work and fix crashes.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location or advertising ID, and we do not sell or share data for advertising.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on the device. You can ask us to delete the analytics data tied to your installation by email.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps the state of the puzzles you have started, which daily editions you have solved, your streak, whether you have completed the guided first puzzle, and a one-question rating if you chose to answer it. This data never leaves the device except through Android's own backup, which is controlled by your Google account settings and is not accessible to us.

What is collected

The app uses two services from Google LLC, both part of Firebase:

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Firebase Analytics App events such as "puzzle started", "hint requested" and "puzzle completed", with the puzzle size, difficulty and hint technique; device model, operating system version, app version, language and country (derived from the IP address by Google; the app itself stores no address); and a random app-instance identifier that is reset when you reinstall the app. To learn which puzzles are too hard, whether hints help, and whether people come back. This decides what we build next.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash and error reports: stack trace, device model, operating system version, free memory and storage, app version, the sequence of app events leading up to the crash, and the same random app-instance identifier. To find and fix crashes.

Google retains Firebase Analytics data at the user level for up to 2 months and aggregated reports for longer; Crashlytics reports are retained for 90 days. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase Data Processing Terms. Google's own privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

What is not collected

  • No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or files.
  • No precise location. The app does not request the location permission.
  • No advertising ID. Advertising-ID collection is switched off in the app's configuration.
  • No data is sold, and none is shared with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Advertising and purchases

The app currently shows no advertising and offers no in-app purchases. If that changes, this policy will be updated first, the date at the top will change, and any advertising partner and the data it receives will be listed here.

Children

The app is a general-audience logic puzzle. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The anonymous analytics described above are the only data involved for anyone.

Your rights and how to delete data

  • Data on the device: clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics and crash data: email us and we will delete the data linked to your app-instance identifier. The identifier is shown nowhere in the app, so tell us roughly when you installed the app and which device you use, and we will handle it from there.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object under the GDPR, and to complain to your data protection authority. Our legal basis for the analytics above is our legitimate interest in improving the app; you can object at any time by email.

Changes

When this policy changes, the effective date at the top is updated. Material changes are also mentioned in the app's release notes.
